About this chart & calculation

This project tracks how closely dotUSD trades to 1 USDT on Polkadot Asset Hub, using liquidity-pool reserves at finalized blocks.

The spot price follows dotUSD → DOT → USDT. We adjust the reserves for token decimals, then multiply the DOT-per-dotUSD ratio by the USDT-per-DOT ratio. Deviation is (price − 1) × 10,000 basis points: 1.001 USDT is +10 bps, or +0.1%.

Colors and ratings describe the absolute deviation from 1 USDT: Excellent ≤ 5 bps, Good ≤ 20, Fair ≤ 50, Poor ≤ 100, and Horrible above 100.

USDT is the reference, so this does not measure the price against US dollars directly. Spot prices exclude swap fees and trade-size slippage. The chart refreshes every six seconds; finalization means the latest point trails the current time. Block timing is estimated, and missing history appears as gaps.
