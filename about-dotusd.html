What is dotUSD?

dotUSD is Polkadot’s native stablecoin, designed to track one US dollar.

It is a protocol-owned asset governed through Polkadot OpenGov, intended for payments, applications and on-chain budgets.

Referendum 1944 also established a DOT/dotUSD trading pool on Asset Hub with treasury-funded liquidity. You can hold dotUSD on-chain without also holding DOT.

Initial phase: USDT backing
Users can create dotUSD by depositing USDT at a one-to-one ratio, subject to a supply cap.
Later phase: DOT collateral
The referendum proposes borrowing dotUSD against DOT collateral, with mechanisms to manage changes in DOT’s value.

Source: Referendum 1944 — dotUSD: A Native Stablecoin for Polkadot, including its phased rollout.
