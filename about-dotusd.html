What is dotUSD?

dotUSD is Polkadot’s native stablecoin, designed to track one US dollar.

Polkadot OpenGov governs dotUSD.

You can hold dotUSD on-chain without also holding DOT.

Phase 1USDT backing
You can mint 1 dotUSD by depositing 1 USDT, up to the supply cap.
Phase 2DOT collateral
The proposal would let you borrow dotUSD using DOT as collateral.

Source: dotUSD proposal.
