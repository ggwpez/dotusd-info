What is dotUSD?

dotUSD is Polkadot’s native stablecoin, designed to track one US dollar. It is a protocol-owned asset governed through Polkadot OpenGov, intended to provide a stable unit for payments, applications and on-chain budgets.

Referendum 1944 introduced a phased rollout. In the initial phase, users can create dotUSD by depositing USDT at a one-to-one ratio, subject to a supply cap. A later phase proposes borrowing dotUSD against DOT collateral, with mechanisms to manage changes in DOT’s value.

The referendum also established a DOT/dotUSD trading pool on Asset Hub with treasury-funded liquidity. dotUSD can be held on-chain without also holding DOT.

Source: Referendum 1944 — dotUSD: A Native Stablecoin for Polkadot, including its phased rollout.

How the price is calculated

The chart shows how far the price of one dotUSD is from 1 USDT.

To calculate that price, we use DOT as a common reference. The Asset Hub DEX reports what one DOT is worth in USDT and what one DOT is worth in dotUSD. Dividing those two prices tells us what one dotUSD is worth in USDT.

USDT per dotUSD=USDT per DOT ÷ dotUSD per DOT

For example, if one DOT is worth 1.998 USDT or 2 dotUSD, then one dotUSD is worth 1.998 ÷ 2 = 0.999 USDT. That puts dotUSD slightly below its peg.

Qualifying trade
A swap with at least 10,000 of each token’s smallest units on each side: 0.000001 DOT and 0.01 USDT or dotUSD. This dust filter excludes tiny swaps whose prices are distorted by rounding.

How tight is the peg?

The reference is 1 dotUSD = 1 USDT. A basis point (bps) is 0.01%; 100 bps equals 1%.

Deviation in bps = (price − 1) × 10,000

Positive values mean dotUSD trades above USDT; negative values mean it trades below. A price of 0.999 is −10 bps. The rating uses the absolute deviation, so +10 and −10 bps receive the same rating.

What each sample represents

The live chart checks for updates every 6 seconds. An observation can repeat the previous price: a refresh does not imply a new trade or a newly finalized block.

Historical samples reconstruct the DOT/USDT and DOT/dotUSD prices from finalized Asset Hub swaps, using the same trade filter, 24-hour window and reserve fallback. They follow the normal chart’s block range and estimated timestamps.

The latest trades in those two markets may have happened at different times. That is why this last-trade calculation can differ from the main chart’s pool reserve calculation, which reads both pools at the same finalized block.

History is imported gradually in small batches. Newly imported history appears on a periodic refresh. These prices are not execution quotes for a new swap.
