How the price is calculated

Both tickers express a token amount per DOT. Dividing them cancels out DOT and gives the price of one dotUSD in USDT.

USDT per dotUSD=USDT per DOT ÷ dotUSD per DOT

Example: 1.998 USDT per DOT ÷ 2 dotUSD per DOT = 0.999 USDT per dotUSD.

Ticker
A market snapshot for a trading pair. We use the Asset Hub DEX’s DOT/USDT and DOT/dotUSD tickers.
Base & quote
DOT is the base token in both pairs. USDT and dotUSD are the quote tokens: the units in which each DOT price is expressed.
Last price
The most recent qualifying trade within the previous 24 hours. If none qualifies, the DEX uses the current pool reserve ratio.
Qualifying trade
A swap with at least 10,000 of each token’s smallest units on each side: 0.000001 DOT and 0.01 USDT or dotUSD. This dust filter excludes tiny swaps whose prices are distorted by rounding.

Source: Asset Hub DEX tickers · Calculation rules. This page uses DEX data only; it does not call CoinGecko.

How tight is the peg?

The reference is 1 dotUSD = 1 USDT. A basis point (bps) is 0.01%; 100 bps equals 1%.

Deviation in bps = (price − 1) × 10,000

Positive values mean dotUSD trades above USDT; negative values mean it trades below. A price of 0.999 is −10 bps. The rating uses the absolute deviation, so +10 and −10 bps receive the same rating.

These are this site’s rating thresholds. The comparison measures the peg against USDT, not an independent US-dollar reference.

What each sample represents

The live chart checks for updates every 6 seconds. An observation can repeat the previous price: a refresh does not imply a new trade or a newly finalized block.

Historical samples reconstruct both ticker legs from finalized Asset Hub swaps, using the same trade filter, 24-hour window and reserve fallback. They follow the normal chart’s block range and estimated timestamps.

The two latest trades may have happened at different times. That is why this last-trade calculation can differ from the main chart’s pool reserve calculation, which reads both pools at the same finalized block.

History is imported gradually in small batches. Newly imported history appears on a periodic refresh. These prices are not execution quotes for a new swap.
