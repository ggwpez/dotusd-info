What is dotUSD?

dotUSD is Polkadot’s native stablecoin, designed to track one US dollar.

It is a protocol-owned asset governed through Polkadot OpenGov, intended for payments, applications and on-chain budgets.

Referendum 1944 also established a DOT/dotUSD trading pool on Asset Hub with treasury-funded liquidity. You can hold dotUSD on-chain without also holding DOT.

Initial phase: USDT backing
Users can create dotUSD by depositing USDT at a one-to-one ratio, subject to a supply cap.
Later phase: DOT collateral
The referendum proposes borrowing dotUSD against DOT collateral, with mechanisms to manage changes in DOT’s value.

Source: Referendum 1944 — dotUSD: A Native Stablecoin for Polkadot, including its phased rollout.

How the price is calculated

The chart shows how far the price of one dotUSD is from 1 USDT.

To calculate that price, we use DOT as a common reference. The Asset Hub DEX reports what one DOT is worth in USDT and what one DOT is worth in dotUSD. Dividing those two prices tells us what one dotUSD is worth in USDT.

USDT per dotUSD=USDT per DOT ÷ dotUSD per DOT

For example, if one DOT is worth 1.998 USDT or 2 dotUSD, then one dotUSD is worth 1.998 ÷ 2 = 0.999 USDT. That puts dotUSD slightly below its peg.

Base & quote
DOT is the base token in both pairs. USDT and dotUSD are the quote tokens: the units in which each DOT price is expressed.
Last price
The most recent qualifying trade within the previous 24 hours. If none qualifies, the DEX uses the current pool reserve ratio.
Qualifying trade
A swap with at least 10,000 of each token’s smallest units on each side: 0.000001 DOT and 0.01 USDT or dotUSD. This dust filter excludes tiny swaps whose prices are distorted by rounding.

How tight is the peg?

The reference is 1 dotUSD = 1 USDT. A basis point (bps) is 0.01%; 100 bps equals 1%.

Deviation in bps = (price − 1) × 10,000

Positive values mean dotUSD trades above USDT; negative values mean it trades below. A price of 0.999 is −10 bps. The rating uses the absolute deviation, so +10 and −10 bps receive the same rating.

These are this site’s rating thresholds. The comparison measures the peg against USDT, not an independent US-dollar reference.

What each sample represents

Live updates · every 6 seconds
A sample records the latest available price. It can repeat the previous value: a refresh does not imply a new trade or a newly finalized block.
Historical samples
Both market prices are reconstructed from finalized Asset Hub swaps, using the same trade filter, 24-hour window and reserve fallback. Samples follow the normal chart’s block range and estimated timestamps.
Why the charts can differ
The latest DOT/USDT and DOT/dotUSD trades may have happened at different times. The main chart instead uses both pools’ reserves at the same finalized block.

History is imported gradually in small batches. Newly imported history appears on a periodic refresh. These prices are not execution quotes for a new swap.
